Manufacturer of Bagasse Products

Growood India is a leading manufacturer of eco-friendly Bagasse Products. Our Bagasse Products are made from renewable sugarcane pulp, which is a byproduct of the sugar industry. By using Bagasse Products, you can help reduce your carbon footprint and protect the planet for future generations.

Our Bagasse Products are biodegradable, compostable, and microwave-safe, making them ideal for a wide range of applications. Whether you need disposable plates, bowls, cups, or takeout containers, we have you covered. Our products are sturdy, durable, and can withstand hot and cold temperatures without leaking or breaking. They are perfect for use in restaurants, cafes, hotels,schools, and other food service establishments.
