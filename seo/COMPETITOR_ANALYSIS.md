# Competitor SEO Analysis & Strategy

## 1. BAGASSEPACKAGING.CO.UK (🌟 UK-Based – Most Advanced)

### Core Strategy
- **Target**: UK + EU wholesale market
- **Focus**: EN13432 certification, PFAS-free claims, B2B wholesale
- **Positioning**: London-based importer/wholesaler for UK & EU

### Metadata & Keywords Strategy
**Title Tag** (68 chars):
```
Bagasse Packaging UK | EN13432 Certified Compostable Food Packaging Supplier London
```
**Keywords**:
- bagasse packaging UK
- compostable food packaging supplier London
- EN13432 certified tableware UK
- eco-friendly takeaway containers wholesale
- biodegradable plates UK supplier
- PFAS-free food containers
- sustainable packaging importer UK
- bagasse tableware wholesaler
- sugarcane packaging UK
- compostable packaging B2B UK

**Meta Description** (158 chars):
```
UK's leading supplier of EN13432 certified bagasse packaging. RIPPLE18 LTD imports, supplies & wholesales eco-friendly compostable plates, bowls, containers. PFAS-free, 100% biodegradable. Serving UK restaurants, caterers & EU markets. London-based. Get wholesale pricing today.
```

### Schema Implementation (🔥 Most Sophisticated)
1. **Organization Schema** - Enhanced with:
   - Multiple `areaServed` (UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Ireland, Italy, Spain)
   - Multiple `ContactPoint` (Sales + Support)
   - `availableLanguage` list (English, German, French, Spanish)
   - `hasOfferCatalog` with itemized products
   - `sameAs` links (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)

2. **WebSite Schema** - With SearchAction for internal site search

3. **WebPage Schema** - Includes:
   - `speakable` schema for voice search (targets h1, hero-subtitle, hero-description)
   - `datePublished` and `dateModified`
   - `mainEntity` as ItemList of product categories

4. **LocalBusiness Schema** - For geo-targeting:
   - Street address, postal code, coordinates
   - Opening hours (9:00 AM – 5:30 PM, weekdays)
   - `priceRange` (££)

5. **FAQPage Schema** - For featured snippets

### Technical SEO Plays
- **Geo meta tags**: `geo.region`, `geo.placename`, `geo.position`, `ICBM`
- **Target audience meta**: `audience: B2B, Restaurants, Caterers, Retailers, Wholesalers, Distributors`
- **Distribution meta**: Coverage for UK, EU, specific countries
- **Open Graph + Twitter Card**: Rich social previews
- **DNS prefetch + preconnect**: Performance optimization for CDNs
- **Favicon + Apple touch icon**: Brand trust signals
- **Canonical URL**: Duplicate prevention

### Page Structure
- Top header bar with key info (green background, eco branding)
- Sticky navigation (navbar)
- Hero section with gradient overlay
- Trust bar with certifications
- "What is Bagasse" educational section
- Product categories grid
- "Why Choose Us" benefits section
- Stats section (proof)
- "Markets We Serve" section (geo-focused)
- CTA section
- Rich footer with links to internal pages

### Internal Linking Strategy
- Footer links to: About, Export Services, Certifications, Sustainability, Contact, Knowledge Center, Blog, Case Studies, FAQs
- Breadcrumb navigation visible
- Category pages linked from homepage
