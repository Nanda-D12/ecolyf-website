Bagasse Food Containers

Ekoroll Bagasse Food Containers are compostable sugarcane packaging solutions designed for takeaway, food delivery and horeca operations seeking a scalable, plastic-free alternative to conventional food packaging. Made from sugarcane fiber, a natural byproduct of sugar production, these containers offer the performance of conventional food packaging without the environmental cost.

As EU packaging regulations continue to restrict single-use plastics and foam, bagasse food containers have become the preferred choice for distributors, food service brands and horeca operators building sustainable packaging programs.

For a complete plastic-free packaging system, combine bagasse containers with our lid-free hot paper cups, molded fiber lids and lid-free cold paper cups.

What Are Bagasse Food Containers?

Bagasse food containers are single-use packaging products made from compressed sugarcane fiber. After sugarcane is processed for sugar, the remaining fibrous pulp is dried, molded and formed into durable food containers. The result is a packaging solution that is fully compostable, petroleum-free and plastic-free.

Compared to plastic, foam and conventional paper containers, bagasse packaging offers better heat resistance, structural durability and a significantly lower environmental footprint. It is rapidly becoming the standard for takeaway and delivery operations transitioning to sustainable packaging.

Technical Performance

Ekoroll bagasse food containers are engineered for real-world food service conditions. Each container is tested for performance across the full range of temperatures and conditions found in professional horeca and takeaway operations.

  • Microwave safe for reheating directly in the container
  • Oven safe for warming food without transfer
  • Grease resistant up to 95°C for hot and fatty food items
  • Water resistant up to 100°C for soups, sauces and hot liquids
  • Refrigerator and freezer safe for storage and meal prep operations
  • 90-day biodegradable under industrial composting conditions
  • Stackable and lightweight for efficient bulk storage and logistics

Certifications and Compliance

Ekoroll bagasse food containers meet international compostability and food safety standards. All containers are compliant for use in European and global markets.

  • EN13432 — European compostability standard for industrial composting
  • OK COMPOST — certified compostable under industrial and home composting conditions
  • BPI Certified — Biodegradable Products Institute certification for North American markets
  • FDA compliant — food contact safety approved for direct food use
  • Plastic-free and petroleum-free construction throughout
  • EU SUP Directive aligned — compliant with EU Single-Use Plastics regulations

Why Businesses Switch to Bagasse Packaging

The shift away from plastic and foam food containers is being driven by both regulation and buyer preference across European markets. Bagasse food containers address both, offering a practical and scalable solution that meets EU requirements without compromising on performance.

  • Fully compostable and biodegradable in 90 days under industrial conditions
  • Plastic-free and petroleum-free throughout the entire product
  • Performs across hot, cold, oily and liquid food applications
  • Compliant with EU Single-Use Plastics Directive and upcoming packaging regulations
  • Supports sustainability goals and ESG reporting for food service brands
  • Reduces plastic waste liability for horeca operators and distributors

Bagasse vs Plastic and Foam

For businesses evaluating sustainable packaging alternatives, bagasse consistently outperforms conventional materials on both environmental and regulatory criteria.

  • Bagasse: compostable, renewable, food-safe, EU-compliant and petroleum-free
  • Plastic: non-compostable, petroleum-based, subject to increasing EU restrictions and bans
  • Foam (EPS): non-recyclable, banned or restricted in many European markets, high environmental impact
