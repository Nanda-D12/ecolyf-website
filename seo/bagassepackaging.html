What is Bagasse?

The sustainable alternative to plastic and paper disposables

Natural Fiber from Sugarcane

Bagasse is the fibrous residue remaining after sugarcane stalks are crushed to extract juice. Instead of being burned or discarded, this agricultural byproduct is transformed into eco-friendly tableware through a molding process using heat and pressure—without any chemical additives or plastic coatings.

Key Benefits:

  • 100% Compostable

    Breaks down in 60-90 days in commercial composting

  • Microwave & Freezer Safe

    Heat resistant up to 200°F (95°C)

  • Oil & Grease Resistant

    No plastic or PFAS coatings needed

  • Sturdy & Durable

    Holds hot, cold, wet, and oily foods
